Help & Safety

Contact us: [support email]
We reply within 24 hours.

Report a memory or a member

Open the memory and tap ••• → Report, or open Trip Options (•••) and tap the member. Reports are confidential. We review them within 24 hours. Reporting a memory hides it for you straight away.

Block someone

Tap ••• on one of their memories, or tap them in Trip Options, and choose Block. You won’t see their photos or videos in any trip. They aren’t notified.

Remove a member (organisers)

In Trip Options, tap the member and choose Remove from trip. Everything they added to that trip is deleted and they can’t rejoin with an old link.

Why can’t I see my photos?

That’s the point of L8R: everything stays sealed, for everyone, until the trip ends. When the reveal time passes, the trip turns into a shared gallery.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you added. See the Privacy Policy for details.

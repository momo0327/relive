Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Relive (“we”, “us”) is operated by Zenia Digital, Sweden. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Relive app and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name, email address and an account ID. With Apple’s “Hide My Email” we receive a private relay address instead.
  • Profile: the display name, avatar colour and optional profile photo you choose.
  • Trips and memories: trip names, dates and cover images, and the photos and videos you add to trips, with the time they were captured.
  • Safety information: reports you submit and people you block.

We do not collect your location. Photos are re-encoded on your device before upload, which removes embedded location data. Videos chosen from your library may still contain location data recorded by your camera; only members of the trip can access them after the reveal. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How memories are protected

Photos and videos are stored privately. Until a trip’s reveal time, nobody can view or download them, including you. After the reveal, only members of that trip can view them, through links that expire after a few minutes. Members can save memories to their own devices after the reveal.

How we use information

Only to run Relive: to show your trips to the people you invite, to reveal memories when a trip ends, to let you manage your account, and to keep the service safe (reviewing reports and enforcing blocks and removals). We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in on our behalf (servers in the EU, Ireland).
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • Resend delivers safety-report notifications to our team.
  • Unsplash provides the optional cover photos you can search for when creating a trip; your search words and device IP address are sent to Unsplash when you use it.

Other trip members can see your display name and profile photo. They never see your email address.

Retention and deletion

  • If you delete a memory, leave a trip, or are removed from one, the photos and videos you added to that trip are permanently deleted.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account). This permanently deletes your account, profile and every photo and video you added. Trips you organise pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the only one.
  • Deleted files are removed from storage within about 30 minutes. Database backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 7 days.
  • Reports may be kept without your identity attached to keep the service safe.

Your choices and rights

You control camera, microphone and photo access in iOS Settings. You can update your profile or delete your account at any time in the app. To request a copy of your data or ask a question, contact mohamed@zeniadigital.se. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA or UK), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

Relive is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.
